1. Executive Summary

The Emirates Digital Bank application currently operates on a conventional Western banking backend. Despite its UAE branding and Arabic-language user interface, the underlying platform lacks the Islamic finance product structures, Middle East regulatory reporting capabilities, regional payment network integrations, and KYC processes mandated by the Central Bank of the UAE (CBUAE), the Saudi Arabian Monetary Authority (SAMA), and the wider GCC regulatory framework.

This User Requirement Specification defines the full set of requirements to make the Emirates Digital Bank fully operational in the Middle East — primarily the UAE and KSA, with extensibility to the broader GCC region. Requirements are organized into six workstreams (WS1–WS6) spanning Islamic finance products, regulatory compliance, KYC/identity verification, regional payment networks, multi-currency and remittance, and Arabic/RTL front-end support.

Implementation is phased to manage dependencies:

  • Phase 1 (Independent): WS3 (KYC), WS5 (Multi-Currency), WS6 (Arabic/RTL)
  • Phase 2 (Depends on Phase 1): WS1 (Islamic Finance), WS4 (Payment Networks)
  • Phase 3 (Depends on Phase 1 + 2): WS2 (Regulatory Compliance)

2. WS1 — Islamic Finance Products Critical

This workstream introduces Shariah-compliant financial products to replace or augment the existing conventional loan and investment instruments. All products must comply with AAOIFI standards and receive Shariah Supervisory Board (SSB) approval before customer availability.

Req IDRequirementPriority
REQ-IF-001System shall support Murabaha (cost-plus) financing with configurable markup rates. The bank purchases an asset and resells it to the customer at a disclosed profit margin; payment may be deferred or in installments.Critical
REQ-IF-002System shall support Ijara (leasing) with rental schedule generation. The bank purchases and leases an asset to the customer; ownership may transfer at the end of the lease (Ijara wa Iqtina).Critical
REQ-IF-003System shall support Diminishing Musharaka for home financing with equity split tracking. The bank and customer co-own the property; the customer gradually buys out the bank's share.Critical
REQ-IF-004System shall support Mudarabah profit-sharing investment accounts with configurable profit ratios between the bank (Mudarib) and the customer (Rabb-ul-Maal).Critical
REQ-IF-005System shall replace interest-rate calculations with profit-rate calculations for all Islamic products. No reference to "interest" shall appear in Islamic product workflows.Critical
REQ-IF-006System shall support Shariah Supervisory Board (SSB) approval workflow for new products — including submission, review, approval/rejection, and audit trail.Critical
REQ-IF-007System shall support Wadiah (safekeeping) current accounts. Funds are held in trust; the bank may use them with implicit permission but guarantees return on demand.Critical
REQ-IF-008System shall support Wakala (agency) investment contracts. The customer appoints the bank as agent (Wakeel) to invest funds for an agreed fee.Critical
REQ-IF-009System shall support Sukuk (Islamic bonds) in the investment service. Sukuk represent ownership in tangible assets or services; the system must track underlying assets and profit distribution.Critical
REQ-IF-010System shall support Takaful (Islamic insurance) product references. While underwriting is external, the system must store policy references, premium schedules, and claim statuses.Critical

3. WS2 — Middle East Regulatory Compliance Critical

This workstream adds regulatory reporting and tax compliance capabilities specific to the UAE, KSA, and GCC jurisdictions. The existing BSA/SAR/CTR reporting framework must be extended — not replaced — to support parallel Middle East reporting.

Req IDRequirementPriority
REQ-RC-001System shall generate goAML suspicious transaction reports per CBUAE format. Reports must conform to the goAML XML schema and be submittable to the CBUAE Financial Intelligence Unit.Critical
REQ-RC-002System shall generate CBUAE prudential returns including capital adequacy, liquidity ratios, and large exposure reports.Critical
REQ-RC-003System shall support MENAFATF compliance frameworks including risk-based approach assessments aligned with FATF and MENAFATF mutual evaluation criteria.Critical
REQ-RC-004System shall implement UAE Federal Decree-Law No. 20/2018 AML/CFT reporting including suspicious transaction reports, threshold-based reports, and cross-border wire transfer reports.Critical
REQ-RC-005System shall calculate Zakat at 2.5% on eligible assets with nisab threshold check. Non-zakatable assets (personal use items, primary residence) must be excluded from calculation.Critical
REQ-RC-006System shall handle 5% GCC VAT on applicable banking fees. VAT-exempt transactions (e.g., Islamic finance profit margins treated as financial supplies) must be correctly identified.Critical
REQ-RC-007System shall support SAMA reporting for KSA operations including prudential returns and AML reporting in the Saudi format.Critical
REQ-RC-008System shall use AED 35,000 CTR threshold (not USD 10,000) for UAE currency transaction reporting. The threshold must be configurable per jurisdiction.Critical

4. WS3 — KYC / Identity Verification Critical

This workstream adds UAE and KSA-specific identity documents, eKYC channels, and beneficial ownership verification to the existing KYC framework.

Req IDRequirementPriority
REQ-KY-001System shall validate Emirates ID format (784-YYYY-NNNNNNN-C) and integrate with ICP (UAE Identity Authority) for real-time verification. Checksum validation and expiry date enforcement are mandatory.Critical
REQ-KY-002System shall support eKYC via UAE Pass (OAuth-based digital identity). UAE Pass provides Level 2 identity assurance; the system must handle token exchange, identity assertion parsing, and session binding.Critical
REQ-KY-003System shall support Iqama (residency permit) for expat customers. The Iqama number and expiry must be validated and stored as a primary identity document for non-citizen residents.Critical
REQ-KY-004System shall verify Trade License for business account onboarding. Trade license number, issuing authority (DED, ADGM, DIFC), and expiry date must be captured and validated.Critical
REQ-KY-005System shall integrate with UBO (Beneficial Ownership) registry per CBUAE requirements. Ultimate beneficial owners with 25%+ ownership must be identified and screened.Critical
REQ-KY-006System shall support Saudi Absher National ID integration for KSA customer onboarding and identity verification.Critical

5. WS4 — Regional Payment Networks High

This workstream integrates the platform with UAE and GCC domestic and cross-border payment clearing systems.

Req IDRequirementPriority
REQ-PN-001System shall integrate with UAEFTS (UAE Funds Transfer System) for RTGS (Real-Time Gross Settlement) transfers. Messages must conform to CBUAE ISO 20022 format.High
REQ-PN-002System shall integrate with UAEIPS for real-time instant payments. Transfers must settle within 10 seconds and support IBAN-based addressing.High
REQ-PN-003System shall support WPS (Wage Protection System) with SIF file generation per Ministry of Labour (MoL) format. Batch salary processing, validation, and submission tracking are required.High
REQ-PN-004System shall integrate with SADAD for Saudi bill payment. Bill presentment, payment, and reconciliation must be supported.High
REQ-PN-005System shall integrate with SARIE for Saudi real-time payments, supporting instant domestic transfers within KSA.High
REQ-PN-006System shall support GCC-RTGS and AFAQ cross-border payment systems for inter-GCC settlements.High

6. WS5 — Multi-Currency & Remittance High

This workstream introduces multi-currency wallet capabilities and dedicated remittance corridors for the large expatriate population in the GCC.

Req IDRequirementPriority
REQ-MC-001System shall support multi-currency wallets for AED, SAR, QAR, BHD, OMR, and KWD. Each wallet is a sub-account under the customer's primary account with independent balance tracking.High
REQ-MC-002System shall integrate with CBUAE reference rates for real-time foreign exchange. Rates must be refreshed at configurable intervals (default: 15 minutes).High
REQ-MC-003System shall support dedicated remittance corridors for India, Pakistan, Philippines, and Bangladesh — the four largest remittance destinations from the UAE.High
REQ-MC-004System shall calculate corridor-specific fees and apply AML limits per corridor. Each corridor has distinct fee structures, exchange rate spreads, and regulatory limits.High

7. WS6 — Arabic Language & RTL Support High

This workstream completes the Arabic localization and RTL rendering for the web and mobile front-ends.

Req IDRequirementPriority
REQ-AR-001System shall support full RTL (right-to-left) layout for Arabic UI. All components must mirror correctly — navigation, forms, tables, modals, and charts.High
REQ-AR-002System shall provide complete Arabic translations. The existing es.json (mislabeled — contains Arabic content) must be renamed to ar.json and validated for completeness against en.json.High
REQ-AR-003System shall support Hijri calendar display alongside Gregorian. Date fields must show both calendars when the Arabic locale is active.High
REQ-AR-004System shall format numbers using Arabic-Indic numerals (٠١٢٣٤٥٦٧٨٩) when the Arabic locale is active, using Intl.NumberFormat('ar-AE').High

9. Acceptance Criteria

WS1 — Islamic Finance Products

Req IDAcceptance Criteria
REQ-IF-001Given a Murabaha financing request with asset cost X and markup rate R%, the system calculates total price = X * (1 + R/100) and generates an installment schedule. The profit amount is disclosed to the customer before contract signing.
REQ-IF-002Given an Ijara lease request, the system generates a rental schedule with monthly payments for the lease term. The asset remains on the bank's balance sheet until ownership transfer (if Ijara wa Iqtina).
REQ-IF-003Given a Diminishing Musharaka contract with initial equity split (e.g., 80/20 bank/customer), the system tracks equity reduction per payment period and recalculates rental payments on the bank's diminishing share.
REQ-IF-004Given a Mudarabah investment with a profit-sharing ratio (e.g., 60/40), the system distributes realized profits according to the agreed ratio. Losses are borne entirely by the capital provider (customer).
REQ-IF-005When an Islamic product is processed, no interest-rate field or interest-based calculation is invoked. Attempting to apply interest to an Islamic product throws a ShariahViolationException.
REQ-IF-006A new Islamic product cannot be offered to customers until an SSB member approves it through the workflow. The audit trail records submitter, reviewer, decision, and timestamp.
REQ-IF-007A Wadiah current account can be opened, deposits and withdrawals function on demand, and no profit/interest is credited to the account holder (hibah/gift is optional and at bank's discretion).
REQ-IF-008A Wakala investment contract is created with an agreed management fee. The bank invests funds as agent and returns profits (minus fee) to the customer.
REQ-IF-009Sukuk instruments can be listed, purchased, and tracked in the investment portfolio. Profit distributions are calculated based on the underlying asset performance.
REQ-IF-010Takaful policy references can be stored with policy number, provider, premium schedule, and claim status. Integration hooks allow external Takaful providers to update claim statuses.

WS2 — Middle East Regulatory Compliance

Req IDAcceptance Criteria
REQ-RC-001The system generates a goAML XML report that passes CBUAE schema validation. The report includes all mandatory fields: reporting entity, subject, transaction details, and suspicion indicators.
REQ-RC-002CBUAE prudential return reports are generated with correct capital adequacy ratios, liquidity coverage ratios, and large exposure details in the required format.
REQ-RC-003The system's AML risk assessment methodology aligns with MENAFATF recommendations. Risk categories, scoring, and mitigation actions are documented and auditable.
REQ-RC-004All reports mandated under UAE Federal Decree-Law No. 20/2018 can be generated, including STRs (Suspicious Transaction Reports), threshold reports, and cross-border wire reports.
REQ-RC-005Given eligible assets of value V and nisab threshold N: if V >= N, Zakat = V * 0.025; if V < N, Zakat = 0. Non-zakatable assets are excluded from V.
REQ-RC-006Banking fees are subjected to 5% VAT. VAT-exempt transactions (e.g., certain Islamic finance margins) are correctly excluded. VAT invoices are generated per UAE FTA requirements.
REQ-RC-007SAMA-format reports can be generated for KSA operations, including capital adequacy, AML, and compliance reports.
REQ-RC-008Currency Transaction Reports are triggered at AED 35,000 (not USD 10,000) for UAE operations. The threshold is configurable per jurisdiction.

WS3 — KYC / Identity Verification

Req IDAcceptance Criteria
REQ-KY-001Emirates ID in format 784-YYYY-NNNNNNN-C passes validation. Invalid formats, expired IDs, and failed checksums are rejected with specific error codes.
REQ-KY-002A customer can authenticate via UAE Pass OAuth flow. The system exchanges the authorization code for identity assertions and binds the verified identity to the customer profile.
REQ-KY-003Iqama documents are accepted in the KYC workflow for expat customers. The Iqama number, expiry date, and sponsor details are validated and stored.
REQ-KY-004Business account onboarding requires a valid Trade License. The system verifies the license number, issuing authority, activity codes, and expiry date.
REQ-KY-005For corporate accounts, UBOs with 25%+ ownership are identified, screened against sanctions lists, and recorded per CBUAE requirements.
REQ-KY-006Saudi customers can verify their identity through Absher National ID integration. The system validates the National ID and retrieves verified identity data.

WS4 — Regional Payment Networks

Req IDAcceptance Criteria
REQ-PN-001UAEFTS RTGS transfers are submitted in ISO 20022 format. The system handles acknowledgements, rejections, and settlement confirmations from CBUAE.
REQ-PN-002UAEIPS instant payments settle within the 10-second SLA. IBAN validation is performed before submission. Real-time status updates are provided to the customer.
REQ-PN-003WPS SIF files are generated per MoL format specification. The file includes employer details, employee records, salary amounts, and bank routing. Batch validation ensures all salaries can be funded before submission.
REQ-PN-004SADAD bill presentment retrieves outstanding bills for a customer. Payments are processed through SADAD and reconciliation records are maintained.
REQ-PN-005SARIE instant payments within KSA are processed in real-time with proper format compliance and status tracking.
REQ-PN-006GCC-RTGS and AFAQ cross-border transfers are processed with proper message formatting, correspondent bank routing, and settlement tracking.

WS5 — Multi-Currency & Remittance

Req IDAcceptance Criteria
REQ-MC-001A customer can hold balances in AED, SAR, QAR, BHD, OMR, and KWD simultaneously. Each currency wallet has independent balance, transaction history, and statement generation.
REQ-MC-002FX rates are sourced from CBUAE reference rates, refreshed every 15 minutes (configurable). Conversion uses mid-rate plus configurable spread per currency pair.
REQ-MC-003Remittance to India, Pakistan, Philippines, and Bangladesh corridors is supported with corridor-specific processing, compliance checks, and beneficiary bank validation.
REQ-MC-004Each corridor has defined fee structures and AML limits. Transfers exceeding corridor limits are flagged for enhanced due diligence. Fee transparency is provided before transaction confirmation.

WS6 — Arabic Language & RTL Support

Req IDAcceptance Criteria
REQ-AR-001When the Arabic locale is active, the entire UI renders in RTL mode. Navigation, forms, tables, and modals mirror correctly. No LTR artifacts remain.
REQ-AR-002ar.json contains translations for 100% of the keys in en.json. No untranslated keys fall back to English when the Arabic locale is selected.
REQ-AR-003Date fields display Hijri dates alongside Gregorian when the Arabic locale is active. The conversion is accurate to within one day (due to lunar observation variance).
REQ-AR-004Numeric values display using Arabic-Indic numerals (e.g., ١٢٣٤٥) when the Arabic locale is active. Currency amounts, account numbers, and dates all use the correct numeral system.
